\title{Emitted Effects of Dual Writes:\\
  A Mechanized Two-Tier Recovery Study}
\author{Andreas Andreakis}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
  This is the emitted-effect tier (tier~2) of the dual-write development. It
  builds a conservative \emph{wrapper machine} over the locked
  \isa{Dual{\isacharunderscore}Write{\isacharunderscore}Core} execution layer
  in which every downstream publish is an externally visible \emph{emission}
  recorded in an append-only ledger, and recovery's re-drive is itself emitting
  work driven by a persisted consumer cursor. The two structural laws of the
  wrapper are that it \emph{simulates} the core (every effect step projects to a
  landed core step) and that its emission-level hazard predicate
  \isa{effect{\isacharunderscore}unsafe} is \emph{monotone} along every step and
  reconcile on the reachable states --- once unsafe, no continuation heals the
  ledger. This is the deliberate contrast to the landed store-tier theorems: the
  durable store heals, the emitted effect does not.

  On the operational (cyclic) wrapper --- the promoted primary, extended with a
  recovery-epoch counter and an explicit resume rule --- the development carries a
  headline law, the \emph{recovery-information dilemma}. A single designed-in pair
  of reachable, effect-safe states with equal cores, equal epochs and divergent
  ledgers defeats every re-drive policy that cannot distinguish its members: the
  policy's own re-drive heals every scoped store mismatch on both members, yet
  duplicates or goes premature on one and loses a committed effect on the other.
  Every store-measured policy (each store-persisted cursor --- subsumption
  machine-checked --- and each store-side dedup filter) and every
  \isa{(core,\ epoch)}-measured policy is defeated; a sink-reading payload-delta
  policy \emph{escapes} (heals, stays effect-safe, loses nothing) under a named
  strictly-ascending-source premise, and provably does not factor through the
  store. Exactly-once effect recovery, in the disclosed sense used here, exists
  and requires reading the sink. A post-freeze dichotomy sharpens this into an
  exhaustive alternative: because the wrapper record has exactly three fields,
  ledger-blindness of a policy is the same thing as
  \isa{(core,\ epoch)}-measurability, so every recovery policy either ignores the
  sink and is defeated or reads it.

  The development is honestly two-tier and stays inside its stated scope. The
  \emph{discipline} (T2.8) is the safe half stated as a for-all theorem: every
  trace whose publishes are justified at fire time and fresh against the ledger,
  and whose reconciles re-drive ledger-fresh, internally distinct suffixes, ends
  effect-safe --- with resume needing no guard, a cycle-exercising non-vacuity
  witness, each guard witnessed load-bearing, and the two-premise slogan form
  refuted. A segment factorization interface re-lands the store-tier corpus per
  resume-free segment by citing the landed proofs verbatim, and per-epoch
  re-landings of the store-tier converse, relay, completeness, tightness and
  recovery-grammar theories follow it as restatement carriers with disclosed
  scope, never upgrades. Suffix freshness, a two-tier executable point
  \emph{decider} (never a schedule executor), and the exactly-once reading round
  out the tier. The sink-reading escape is pinned two-sidedly: it reads more than
  the store, yet an upper-bound theorem shows it needs no more than the recovery
  window's delivered-payload presence --- an upper bound about the landed policy,
  not a minimality or characterization claim.

  Two \emph{robustness certificates} soften the disclosed atomic big-step
  reconcile boundary without extending the machine: a small-step reconcile
  variant on which every cyclic trace embeds and the headline dilemma family
  restates verbatim, and a sink-delta convergence lane that iterates and exports
  the owed batch as a certified point function. A compensable-sinks study locates
  the dilemma's boundary on the compensation axis (irreversibility bites exactly
  as cancellativity of the sink observable; order-aware matching escapes,
  aggregate absorption does not). A deliberately branch-neutral \emph{recovery
  class} abstracts the terminal-inexpressibility argument as a locale of four
  assumptions under which no reachable crashed pair carries the dilemma
  certificate; the terminal machine is certified a member and the cyclic machine
  is certified to exit the class, each witness- and control-backed.

  Throughout, the results keep an \emph{exists-system} polarity: they exhibit a
  designed pair or a defeated policy, never assert that all systems called dual
  writes fail. Soundness is scoped-state equality at the chosen frontier; no
  general transactional atomicity, serializability, isolation, exactly-once
  message delivery in the broker sense, or multidatabase atomic-commit protocol
  is modelled or claimed. Every theorem is conditional on the premises stated in
  its statement. The development is sorry-free and axiom-free and builds under
  \isa{quick{\isacharunderscore}and{\isacharunderscore}dirty}~\isa{=}~false; the
  landed slices are additionally certified oracle-free by dependency probes with
  confirmed-biting negative controls, kept outside these sources, and the main
  classes are inhabited by closed witnesses, so no result is vacuously true.
\end{abstract}
